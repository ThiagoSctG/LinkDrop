# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want an open, welcoming community free from harassment. Everyone should be able to participate regardless of experience, identity, appearance, background, circumstances, religion or technical opinion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- Accept feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and safety.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information and unwelcome sexual conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact channels listed on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on the severity and context.

This code applies to all project spaces and to public interactions where someone is representing the project.
